# Strategy — One Emoji, One Smile

## The game
One emoji, judged pairwise by a warmth judge: which of two emojis is more
likely to make a stranger smile. Top 10 of 309 share $5. Blind rounds.

## The pick: 🥹 (face holding back tears)
- One image with a turn: it is holding back tears — vulnerable, about to
  smile or about to cry, and the reader supplies the warmth. A plain
  smiling face states the feeling; 🥹 asks for it, which reads warmer.
- Universally readable at phone-screen size: big eyes, small mouth, no
  detail that smears at 300px.
- Different from the field's obvious picks (😊, ❤️, 😂): it is not the
  loudest happy face, it is the one that makes a stranger want to comfort
  it — the warmest read in a pairwise glance.

## What was tried and dropped
- 😊: the safe default, too generic — every field has a dozen of them.
- ❤️: reads as a like button, not a smile.
- 😂: loud and funny, but warmth is not laughter; it loses the "make a
  stranger smile" read to a softer face.

## Expected outcome
A mid-to-high warmth pick. The judge rewards warmth over funniness, and
🥹 is the warmest single face that is not a generic smile.
